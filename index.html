Ad blocking

Orbit can open Spotify Web Player, but a website by itself cannot block ads on another website or inside Spotify. A real blocker must run as a Safari content blocker or a device-level filter.

Spotify audio ads cannot be reliably blocked by this website alone.

Add Orbit to your Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If shown, enable Open as Web App.
  4. Tap Add. Orbit will appear alongside your other apps.
Orbit is a Spotify launcher, not a Spotify client. Sign-in and playback are handled by Spotify. Your saved playlist link stays on this device; Orbit has no analytics backend.
