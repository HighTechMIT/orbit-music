Ad and sponsor handling

Ytify uses an alternative audio playback frontend rather than the official YouTube Music player. Its playback and ad behavior depend on that third-party service; Orbit's own JavaScript does not intercept YouTube's network requests.

Ytify includes a SponsorBlock option for skipping sponsor segments in non-music content. To use it, open the player's own Settings and enable its SponsorBlock option. It does not reliably remove ads embedded in music streams, and no third-party frontend is guaranteed to stay available or ad-free.

This is an independent player, not an official Google or YouTube Music app. If you prefer the official service, use the YouTube Music button above; its ads and playback are controlled by YouTube.

Add Orbit to your Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If shown, enable Open as Web App.
  4. Tap Add. Orbit will appear alongside your other apps.
